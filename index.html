02 · Technology

A signature library, read by AI, refreshed at the edge.

Pathogens scatter light in patterns specific to their molecular structure. The portal captures those patterns from exhaled breath and resolves them against a continually growing reference library. No biological material is collected. No identity is stored.

01 · Capture

Capture without contact.

An effluent breath sample passes through the imaging chamber. UV decontaminates between subjects. No biological waste produced at any stage.

02 · Resolve

Resolve to a signature.

A proprietary hyperspectral stack records the scattered light. The system isolates the spectral fingerprint and discards everything that is not signal.

03 · Classify

Classify against the library.

An onboard model matches the fingerprint against a versioned signature library. Known and unknown threats are flagged. Anonymized counts feed outbreak surveillance.

From data to decision

The system extracts a signature. Then it collapses to one answer.

Inside the chamber, a controlled illumination source isolates the sample volume. The capture is plotted against the reference library, classified, and rendered to the operator as a single unambiguous result. Everything else stays under the hood.
